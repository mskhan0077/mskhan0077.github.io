I’m an MS student at the [Indian Institute of Science Education and Research (IISER) Bhopal](https://www.iiserb.ac.in/), pursuing a degree in Electrical Engineering and Computer Science. I am currently a Graduate Researcher in the [Multi-Robot Autonomy (MOON) Lab](https://moonlab.iiserb.ac.in/), where my research lies at the intersection of **field robotics, multi-agent systems, and autonomous decision-making under uncertainty**, with an emphasis on deploying algorithms on real robotic platforms.

My work has primarily focused on marine robotics, where I have designed and implemented autonomous surface vehicles capable of operating in challenging, real-world environments. These projects include adaptive bathymetric mapping using informative planning, collision avoidance for marine vehicles under safety and regulatory constraints, and autonomous systems for environmental monitoring and water-body cleaning. Across these efforts, I have consistently emphasised end-to-end system development, combining perception, planning, and control with extensive simulation and field validation.

Through these experiences, I have developed a strong interest in decision-making under uncertainty and learning-based autonomy for robots operating in complex, dynamic environments. Looking ahead, I aim to pursue a PhD to develop robust, data-efficient methods that enable field robots to adapt online, reason over uncertainty, and make informed decisions in partially observable settings. I am particularly motivated to contribute to research that bridges foundational algorithmic development with real-world deployment, enabling autonomous robotic systems that are both theoretically grounded and practically impactful.

When I’m not working, I enjoy running, cycling, hiking, hitting the gym, and playing football (soccer). These activities help me stay active, focused, and inspired — both in and outside the lab.
