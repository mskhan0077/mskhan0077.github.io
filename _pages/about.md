I recently completed my BS-MS in Electrical Engineering and Computer Science at the [Indian Institute of Science Education and Research (IISER) Bhopal](https://www.iiserb.ac.in/). I am currently a Project Associate I in the [Multi-Robot Autonomy (MOON) Lab](https://moonlab.iiserb.ac.in/), where my research lies at the intersection of **field robotics, multi-agent systems, and autonomous decision-making under uncertainty**, with an emphasis on deploying algorithms on real robotic platforms.

My work has primarily focused on marine robotics, where I have designed and implemented autonomous surface vehicles capable of operating in challenging, real-world environments. These projects include adaptive bathymetric mapping using informative planning, collision avoidance for marine vehicles under safety and regulatory constraints, and autonomous systems for environmental monitoring and water-body cleaning. Across these efforts, I have consistently emphasised end-to-end system development, combining perception, planning, and control with extensive simulation and field validation. I have also worked on COLREGs-compliant collision avoidance for autonomous surface vessels (IEEE MRS 2025), and built AquaRP, a low-cost ASV research platform that these experiments run on.

Field trials are what shaped my interests. Plans that are correct on paper often fail once state estimates are noisy and communication between vehicles is delayed. I am looking to start a PhD on methods that let robot teams adapt online, reason about their own uncertainty, and act safely under partial observability.

When I'm not working, I enjoy running, cycling, hiking, hitting the gym, and playing football (soccer). These activities help me stay active, focused, and inspired — both in and outside the lab.
