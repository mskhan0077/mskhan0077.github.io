We are developing an autonomous surface robot inspired by fish locomotion. Our research focuses on efficient obstacle avoidance and intelligent path planning strategies. Currently, we are exploring imitation learning techniques to enable the robot to navigate autonomously from a starting point to a goal location while safely avoiding obstacles.
