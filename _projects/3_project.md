### Overview

Autonomous cleaning boat was built to autonomously collect floating debris from water bodies like lakes, ponds and swimming pools.

**My role:** Contributed to the design, testing, motion planning and integration of sensing, control and debris collection system.

Version 1 was independently designed and built by me, while Version 2 was developed collaboratively with two teammates.

### The Problem

Cleaning floating waste from water bodies is traditionally labor-intensive and can require expensive or bulky equipment. Our aim was to build a low-cost autonomous boat capable of continuously skimming water surface while avoiding obstacles and staying within a designated cleaning region.
