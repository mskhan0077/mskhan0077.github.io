### Overview

AquaRP is a low-cost Autonomous Surface Vehicle (ASV) research platform developed for real-world marine robotics experiments. The platform integrates a Pixhawk-based flight controller, onboard NVIDIA Jetson computation, GPS, IMU, RGB camera and echosounder to support navigation and field deployment.

**My role:** Contributed to the design, system integration and field testing.

### The Problem

Marine robotics research requires autonomous platforms capable of reliable operation in real-world aquatic environments, but developing such systems can be expensive and difficult to scale. There is a need for a low-cost modular Autonomous Surface Vehicle (ASV) that integrates onboard computation, sensing, control and communication while supporting multi-robot coordination for collaborative marine missions.
